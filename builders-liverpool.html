Building work for Liverpool homes

A lot of Liverpool's housing is Victorian and Edwardian terraces. These homes have plenty of character but often need careful work: rear and side-return extensions, knocking through to open up downstairs rooms, and upgrading tired kitchens and bathrooms without losing the original feel.

Older properties can also hide surprises once work starts, from uneven walls to old lintels. We plan for this from the first visit, check the structure properly, and explain any issues clearly before deciding how to proceed.
