Builders on the Wirral

Cottle Construction works across the Wirral, from Birkenhead and Wallasey to Bebington, Heswall and West Kirby. We take on extensions, renovations, interior projects and landscaping for homeowners who want reliable work and clear communication.

Every job starts with a free visit so we can see the property, talk through what you want and give you a straightforward written quote. Once work starts, we keep you updated and keep the site tidy throughout.

  • Building & Structural – Extensions, conversions, and structural alterations.
  • Interiors & Installations – Kitchen and bathroom design, supply, and installation.
  • Renovations & Maintenance – Full house and room renovations.
  • Landscaping – Patios and paving laid level and secure.

Building work for Wirral homes

The Wirral has everything from Victorian villas in Oxton and Birkenhead to inter-war semis and modern estates. We regularly see homeowners wanting more space through extensions and conversions, or bringing older properties up to date with new kitchens, bathrooms and full renovations.

Gardens are a big part of many Wirral homes, and we build patios, decking and fencing with proper groundwork and drainage. Along the coast, we choose materials that cope with the weather so outdoor work stays looking good.
