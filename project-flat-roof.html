Flat Roof Refurbishment

This flat roof had reached the end of its life, with a worn, patchy surface around the existing roof lantern. We re-covered the roof with new felt, finishing it neatly around the lantern, upstands and edges.

The work was planned around the lantern so it stayed sealed and watertight throughout. Each section of felt was laid and lapped in order, with the joints and edges finished so water runs off the roof properly.

The before and after photos show the difference a new flat roof makes. The finished surface is clean, even and sealed, protecting the room below and giving the roof many more years of life.

Project Tips

A flat roof often needs attention long before it starts to leak. Catching wear early and re-covering the roof properly is usually far cheaper than repairing water damage inside.

  • Check flat roofs for cracking, blistering and standing water.
  • Pay close attention to edges, upstands and around rooflights.
  • Clear debris and moss so water can drain freely.
  • Re-cover the whole roof once patch repairs stop working.
  • Make sure rooflights and lanterns are properly sealed in.

Overview & Challenge

The challenge was renewing the roof covering while keeping the existing roof lantern sealed and protected. We worked carefully around it and finished every edge and joint neatly, leaving a clean, watertight flat roof.
