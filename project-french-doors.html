French Doors & Windows

On this job the old windows came out and the brickwork was opened up so new French doors and windows could be fitted, giving the rooms behind direct access out to the garden.

The new frames are finished in dark grey to suit the brickwork, with French doors in the centre and matching windows either side.

Opening up an existing wall needs careful planning and support, so the structure stays sound while the brickwork is altered. Once the openings were formed, the doors and windows were fitted and sealed in, ready for the outside to be finished.

Project Tips

Swapping windows for French doors can transform how a room is used, but the opening has to be planned properly. Getting the support, sizes and finishes right at the start avoids problems later.

  • Plan any new or enlarged openings and their support before cutting into brickwork.
  • Measure openings accurately and order doors and windows in good time.
  • Choose frame colours that suit the brickwork and the rest of the building.
  • Make sure thresholds and sills are set so water runs away from the building.
  • Seal frames properly once fitted to keep out draughts and rain.

Overview & Challenge

The challenge was altering the existing brickwork safely and fitting the new doors and windows neatly into the openings. The result is a much brighter space with easy access straight out onto the garden.
